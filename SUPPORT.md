# Technical Support

The Microwave Control Firmware project is actively maintained by the core appliance-controls team.

Support requests are monitored every business day, with critical heating or interlock issues receiving a same-day response. Routine questions are answered within three business days.

The current maintenance cycle includes monthly dependency review, quarterly firmware releases, and emergency maintenance releases when a safety issue is identified.

Contact the technical support team through the project service desk or the maintainer channel associated with the official distribution.
